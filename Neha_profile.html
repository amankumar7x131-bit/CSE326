About the Doctor

Professional background and paediatric expertise

Dr. Neha Iyer is presented as an experienced Paediatrician at City Care Hospital, focusing on the medical care and healthy development of infants, children and adolescents.

Her clinical approach emphasizes early diagnosis, preventive healthcare, regular growth monitoring and age-appropriate medical treatment.

She believes that every child deserves individual attention and a comfortable healthcare experience. She also values communication with parents and caregivers to help them understand their child's health and development.
