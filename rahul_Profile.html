About the Doctor

Professional background and medical expertise

Dr. Rahul Mehta is presented as an experienced General Physician at City Care Hospital, specializing in primary healthcare, general medicine and preventive healthcare.

His clinical approach focuses on understanding the patient's medical history, identifying symptoms, conducting appropriate clinical examinations and recommending suitable investigations.

He believes in preventive healthcare, early diagnosis, patient education and long-term health management. His goal is to make quality healthcare accessible and understandable for patients of different age groups.
