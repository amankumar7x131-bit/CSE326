About the Doctor

Professional background and medical expertise

Dr. Asha Sharma is presented as an experienced cardiologist at City Care Hospital. Her clinical focus includes the prevention, diagnosis and management of cardiovascular conditions.

She follows a patient-first approach, emphasizing accurate clinical assessment, evidence-based treatment and clear communication with patients and their families.

Her approach focuses on early diagnosis, regular monitoring, lifestyle guidance and personalized treatment planning according to each patient's medical needs.
